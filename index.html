Quick answers

Before you drop your email in.

Do I have to burn my bunnies?

No. A bunny you never burn still earns carrots daily, still gets rarer as other people burn theirs, and stays tradeable. Burning is the fast exit, not the only path.

What actually happens when I burn?

The NFT is destroyed on chain and $CARROT arrives in your wallet in the same transaction. No claim page, no waiting period, no vesting. You also keep a soulbound Founder Deed showing that bunny's art and ID, so your face stays on the leaderboard.

Why is it limited to one burn per week?

Because without a throttle the whole collection empties in a weekend and the payout pool is gone before most people have even fed a bunny. One per wallet per week keeps the game alive for months.

Can I get fat bunnies faster by paying more?

No. Two servings per bunny per day is a hard cap, so the fastest possible route to Fat is thirty days for everyone. The Golden Feeder increases weight per serving, which is why the VIP pack matters, but nobody can buy their way past the clock.

What do you do with my email and wallet?

Email gets one confirmation and then launch announcements. The wallet goes into the allowlist and the pack snapshot. Nothing else. We never ask you to connect, sign or approve anything to join a waitlist, and any site that does is not us.

Do I need a special gas token?

No. Robinhood Chain runs on plain ETH. There is no separate chain coin and anyone selling you one is scamming you.
